【游戏名称】
权谋之庭 - Court of Shadows

【短描述 / Short Description】(80字以内)
父亲骤逝，你继承艾登堡并追查死因。五章宫廷权谋故事，通往九个主线结局与一个隐藏尾声。

-------------------------------------
【完整描述 / Full Description】
-------------------------------------

父亲骤逝后，你从王都赶回故乡，继承艾登堡。葬礼尚未结束，领地的债务、边境的敌军和父亲死因的疑案已经一同摆在你面前。

贵族要求你履行旧约，教会等待你的表态，王权则要确认新领主是否忠诚。你可以借助其中一方，也可以拒绝他们。每次决定都会关闭一些道路，也会打开另一些道路；无法选择的选项，往往源自你此前的取舍。

核心内容：
- 五章完整故事
- 九个主线结局与一个隐藏尾声
- 南境故事设有五个独立结果
- 权力、财富、信仰、忠诚、声望、谋略六项属性
- 角色关系、领地治理、战斗、制作与随机事件
- 画廊、音乐室、成就、章节选择、收藏品与决策日志
- PC 与 Android 均支持横屏游玩

通关后开启 New Game+，新周目会继承上次结算时20%的权力、财富和谋略。故事选择从头开始，未走过的道路仍需重新争取。

  Steam Store Description (English)

【Title】
Court of Shadows

【Short Description】
After your father's sudden death, inherit Aidenburgh and investigate what killed him. Your decisions open some roads and close others across five chapters.

【Full Description】

Your father dies without warning. You return from the royal capital to inherit Aidenburgh, only to find his debts, a threatened border, and unanswered questions about his death waiting for you.

The nobles expect old promises to be honored. The Church wants a declaration of faith. The Crown wants proof of the new lord's loyalty. You may accept their help or refuse their terms. Each decision opens some roads and closes others, and an opportunity denied in one chapter may decide what remains available later.

KEY FEATURES:

- Five complete chapters of branching court intrigue
- Nine main endings and one hidden epilogue
- The Southern storyline has five separate outcomes
- Six tracked values: Power, Wealth, Faith, Loyalty, Reputation, and Intrigue
- Character relationships, domain management, combat, crafting, and random events
- Gallery, music room, achievements, chapter select, collectibles, and a decision log
- Landscape support on PC and Android

After finishing the story, New Game+ carries over 20% of your final Power, Wealth, and Intrigue. Story decisions begin again and must be earned on the new route.
