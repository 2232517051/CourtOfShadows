【Title】
Court of Shadows

【Short Description】
A medieval court intrigue visual novel. Navigate politics, forge alliances, and shape your destiny across 5 chapters and 5 unique endings.

【Full Description】

You are the newly appointed Lord of Aidenburgh, thrust into a deadly web of noble ambition, religious fervor, and royal machination.

Court of Shadows is a medieval court intrigue visual novel where every choice carries weight. Navigate treacherous political alliances, uncover palace conspiracies, and confront impossible moral dilemmas as you fight to survive — and perhaps to rule.

KEY FEATURES:

- 5 Full Chapters of branching narrative with 3-4 hours of gameplay
- 5 Distinct Endings shaped by your decisions throughout the story
- Six-Dimensional Stat System tracking your political influence, military strength, faith, wealth, intelligence, and charisma
- Dynamic Relationship System where character affinity shifts based on your choices
- 23+ Game Systems including gallery, music room, achievements, chapter select, story recap, route map, and more
- Hidden Achievements & Collectibles reward thorough exploration
- New Game+ unlocks additional content on subsequent playthroughs
- Oil Painting Art Style with a dark royal gold UI theme
- Touch-Optimized for mobile with full desktop support

The game of power has begun. Will you rise to the throne, or fall to the shadows?
